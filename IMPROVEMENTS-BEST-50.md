# Scrapyard Derby — the best 50 improvements

Boiled down from the 100-item analysis of Build 67 (2026-11-14): only real improvements,
in impact order. The 8 items that turned out to be already in Build 67 and the 4 skipped
(netcode prediction, second arena, layout rotation, seasonal reskin) are cut. All 50 below
are implemented in the improved working copy (5 as partials, marked).

4. Guided first match — contextual prompts that dismiss as you do them
5. WebGL failure panel instead of a dead menu over a black canvas
6. "Watch that again" wreck replay, on the over screen and after your own wreck
7. Crash cam guaranteed on wreck-causing hits
8. Slow-mo on your own death in Elimination
9. Countdown camera push-in
10. Hit-direction indicator at the screen edge

12. Nitro earned by dealing damage, not just pickups and air

14. Near-miss bonus
15. Shield pickup, and pickup kinds shuffled across spots each match
16. Hazard telegraphs — crusher and crane warn before they strike
17. Rubber-banding so AI hunts a runaway leader and eases off a struggling player
18. Field size (4/6/9/12) as menu chips, not buried in the tune panel
19. Free-drive mode — clock off, learn a car without betting a match
20. AI routes to repair when hurt, weighted by persona
