## VR / WebXR

1. **The jumbotron never renders in VR** — `updateJumbo` early-returns when `XRS.on` is set (line ~1908), so the yard's big screen is frozen on whatever it last showed before you put the headset on. Render it at the lite rate in VR (it's a 384×216 target; the Quest preset already pays for worse) or drive the screen from the replay buffer only while in VR. (M)
2. **No minimap or opponent arrows in the cockpit** — `VR.drawHud()` draws hull/nitro/score/wrecks, but `drawMinimap` and the off-screen arrows (`#arrows`) are DOM-only. Blit the existing minimap canvas into the HUD panel next to the gauge, and draw arrow chevrons on the HUD's edges from the same data the DOM arrows use. In VR you currently drive blind to everything outside your view. (M)
3. **No countdown in VR** — the 3-2-1 and GO live in the DOM overlay; `drawHud` never shows `game.countdown`. Add the count (and GO) to the HUD panel during countdown, timed off `game.countdown` — the horn sting from round 1 is audible, but a deaf or muted VR player gets no start signal at all. (S)
4. **Hit-direction indicator for VR** — round 1's `#hitDir` arc is a DOM element. Reuse the same `onCollide` direction data to flash a rim arc on the VR vignette sphere (rotate a shader wedge, or tint the vignette per-side) so a VR player can tell where a hit came from. (S)
5. **VR results parity** — the VR over-page (`pageItems`, state 'over') shows score/time/wrecked count only. The round-1 results breakdown (biggest hit, props, best air, accuracy, your best) is DOM-only. Feed the same computed stats into the VR over page. (S)
6. **Family-yard entry inside VR** — the VR main page offers "Host a game" and "Join with code" only; the one-tap family yard (`famOn`/`MP.enterFamily`) is a DOM button. Add "Family yard" to the VR menu so a Quest player can land in the standing yard without leaving the headset to tap the 2D page. (S)
7. **Dynamic resolution in the VR governor** — `VR.govern()` only steps `QUALITY.level` down; `renderer.xr.setFramebufferScaleFactor` is fixed once at session start (0.85 Quest 2 / 1 else) and `setFoveation(1)` is maxed forever. Give govern() resolution and foveation steps (1.0 → 0.85 → 0.7, foveation 1 → 0.7) before it sheds world detail — cheaper frames with the same scene. (M)
8. **VR quality never steps back up** — `govern()` only degrades (`V.slow >= 2` → level+1, never back). A cool room after a hot start stays blurry all session. Add the same sustained-headroom step-up the 2D governor has. (S)
9. **120 Hz on Quest 3** — `enter()`/sessionstart picks 90 on non-Quest-2 or 72; Quest 3 supports 120 Hz frame rates via `supportedFrameRates`. Offer 120 as the first choice on Quest 3 hardware — the derby's fast pans are exactly what high refresh sells. (S)
10. **Passthrough mixed-reality mode** — the session requests `immersive-vr` only. On Quest 3/3S, offer an `immersive-ar` variant: the yard's cars and physics stay, the skybox/ground become your actual room (alpha-blended environment). The single most "show someone the Quest" feature this game could have. (L)
11. **VR spectate / director cam** — a VR player who isn't driving (guest waiting, or just watching a family match) gets the static wreck-hold view. Give spectators the autoplay camera (`AUTO.camCar` logic) from a comfortable fixed seat with a "next car" button — watching a derby in-headset is half the family use case. (M)
12. **A personal replay screen in VR** — round 1 put wreck replays on the over screen (DOM) and the jumbotron; in VR neither is watchable (#1). Add a small cinema panel in the rig that plays `JUMBO.rec` back after your wreck and on the over page. (M)
13. **VR low-health feedback** — round 1's low-hull state leans on the post-processing grade pass, which VR bypasses entirely (`renderer.render` straight, no composer). VR only gets discrete damage buzzes. Drive the comfort vignette's inner radius/alpha from hull % so danger is visible in-headset too. (S)
14. **File a bug report from inside VR** — `openBugReport()` and the bug icon are DOM-only; a VR player who hits a bug must exit VR to report it, losing the moment. Add "Report a bug" to the VR pause page (it can call the same `bugReport()` + `dropUpload`). (S)
15. **Hand-tracking driving** — `enter()` requests `hand-tracking` as an optional feature, but hands are used only for pinch-clicks in the menu. Add a hands mode: pinch-and-hold = throttle, hand tilt = steer (feeding the same `touch` funnel). Controller-free play is the Quest's party trick. (L)
16. **A VR tune page** — in-headset you can change VR settings, mode and car, but not `aiCount`, song, theme, free drive or any of the 34 tune settings; every change means leaving VR. Add a compact settings page to the VR menu covering the Match group + song/theme. (M)
17. **Controller tracking-loss and battery surfacing** — when a controller's pose is lost, `ctrl[h].ok` just goes false and the controller vanishes mid-drive with no message (and a dead battery looks identical). Toast the loss ("RIGHT CONTROLLER LOST") and show controller battery in the pause page where the input source exposes it. (S)
18. **Per-car seat height in VR** — `V.set.height` is one global offset, but `cockpitEye()` differs per car (forklift vs Sandflea sit very differently). Remember the height offset per car type, like round 1's per-car camera memory on the flat screen. (S)

## Multiplayer depth

19. **Host moderation: kick, mute, block** — there is no moderation anywhere: a guest can idle in a slot, spam the horn, or grief all evening and the host's only remedy is ending the game. Add host actions in the lobby/NETLOG list: kick (drops the peer, frees the slot), mute horn/barks per guest, block a `MY_DID` for the session. (M)
20. **Handover should transfer the match, not restart it** — host handover exists (epoch election, `famCmp`), but the new host starts cold: `SCORE` rows, match clock and wreck counts are host-local and lost. Include a compact state pack (scoreboard + `game.time` + mode) in the handover so the match survives the host's phone dying. (M)
21. **Public room discovery** — joining today requires a code, a QR, or the family name. Add an opt-in public-yard index on the existing ntfy pattern (hosts beacon "open yard, N seats" on a directory topic; the join panel lists live public yards). The family-code system stays private; this is the front door for everyone else. (L)
22. **A spectator role** — every joiner takes a car slot (12 max). Let a joiner enter as a pure viewer: no car, no slot, director/jumbotron cameras, scoreboard visible. Parents watching the kids' family match is the obvious use; it also makes streaming a match possible. (M)
23. **Exportable match replays** — `JUMBO.rec` is a 4.5-second ring buffer; nothing about a match can be replayed later or shared. Record a low-rate pose stream (the `snapCar` format at 10 Hz compresses well) into the PLAYLOG record and offer "download replay" — a file Jonas or a kid can load back and watch from any camera. (L)
24. **Family-yard host beacons are unauthenticated** — `famPing` asks on the public ntfy topic `sd-<code>-h` and trusts any well-formed answer; the code is a hash of a guessable family name, so anyone who subscribes can answer first and impersonate the yard's host (fake host, arbitrary snapshots). Sign beacons with a key derived from the yard name + PIN (round 1's PIN exists on the branch) and ignore unsigned answers. (M)
25. **Snapshot trimming can drop wreck events** — `hostFrame` trims packets to 3600 chars by slicing props, then slicing `snap.e` (the event ring: wrecks, announcements). Under prop load a guest can silently miss a wreck event. Give events priority classes: score/wreck events are never trimmed; props and ambient events go first. (S)
26. **Quick-chat wheel for guests** — the only cross-player signal is the horn double-tap ping (round 1). Add a 4-phrase quick chat ("Nice shot!", "Help!", "Rematch?", "One more!") sent as MP events and shown as toasts/subtitles — family-appropriate, no free text, no moderation burden. (S)
27. **Team mode** — `SCORE` rows are per-driver and every match is free-for-all. Add 2v2 / 3v3: teams assigned in the lobby, team totals on the scoreboard, team-coloured nameplates. The derby format is built for it; the scoring change is contained (`SCORE.pts` per team). (L)
28. **Cross-device seat handoff** — `MY_DID` is per-browser storage, so your car on your phone is a stranger to your Quest. Add a one-time handoff code: enter it on the second device and it claims your seat (same car, score, roster slot) while the first device drops to spectator. (M)
29. **Guest input throttle + dedupe** — guests publish input through presence with microtask batching, but a noisy stick can still flood presence updates on the room channel. Clamp guest inputs to the snapshot rate (20/s) and drop unchanged frames. (S)
30. **Nickname hygiene** — `MP.nick()` accepts any 14 characters: a guest can impersonate the host's name or put a slur on the scoreboard and in announced lines. Add a light word filter and a "(guest)" disambiguation when a nick collides with the host's. (S)
31. **Floating nameplates over remote cars** — remote players are identifiable only in the lobby, the scoreboard and (round 1) the minimap. Add toggleable world-space nameplates (the persona/car name + nick) that fade beyond ~40 m, so "hit the pink one" works in the yard itself. (S)
32. **Guest-initiated host-struggle vote** — *new angle on round-1 partial #53:* round 1 prompts the struggling host to hand over; the host may be a kid who ignores it. Let guests vote "host is lagging" (majority of guests) to trigger the same handover offer, with the vote shown in NETLOG. (M)
33. **Transport fallback when the PeerJS broker is down** — online play rides the public PeerJS broker; if it's unreachable, hosting/joining fails outright even though the ntfy relay path (used by the family yard and nearby-QR) works. Fall back to the relay transport with a status line saying so, instead of a dead end. (M)
34. **Family-yard welcome line** — the yard has no identity beyond its name. Let the host set a one-line welcome/MOTD stored with the yard beacon ("Saturday derby — winner picks dessert") shown to everyone on join. (S)
35. **AFK guests get auto-parked** — a guest who puts the phone down keeps a live car (and a slot) forever. After 60 s with no input, hand their car to the AI (`respawnType`/AI drive) until they touch a control — their score freezes while parked. (M)
36. **Snapshot compression** — snapshots are raw JSON (~3.6 KB × 20/s × guests). Run them through `CompressionStream` on the peer transport (with a capability flag in presence, plain JSON as fallback) — the weakest link in most home games is uplink, and this roughly halves it. (M)
37. **Host clock in the snapshot** — guests read `cd` (countdown) and `t` off snapshots, but host slow-mo and frame hitches make the guest countdown judder. Include the host's monotonic sim tick and let guests interpolate the countdown against it. (S)
38. **Per-room car memory for guests** — your car choice persists globally (`derby-car-v3`), so joining Dad's yard in the tank also changes your solo default. Remember the last car per room code and restore it on join. (S)
39. **Expiring join codes** — a shared join link/code works forever; a code posted in a screenshot is a permanent invitation. Add an optional per-session code rotation (host regenerates; old code dies at match end). (S)
40. **Overflow sub-yards** — the field caps at 12 cars; a big family night just excludes people. When a yard fills, offer "Yard B" under the same family name (code suffix), with the lobby showing both. (M)
41. **Best-host suggestion from connection history** — round 1 put a ⚠ on struggling names in the lobby. Go further: NETLOG already collects per-peer fps/rtt history; when the family gathers, suggest the device with the best recent hosting record as tonight's host. (M)
42. **In-match spectating for late joiners** — *completes the unimplemented half of round-1 #55:* a guest who joins an Elimination match mid-round currently waits with nothing. Feed them the host's jumbotron/director view of the live match (read-only camera, scoreboard visible) until the next round starts. (M)

## Progression & meta

43. **A lifetime career screen** — PLAYLOG stores every match on the device and the perf panel aggregates a session's totals, but there's no player-facing career view. Build one from the stored records: matches, wrecks, wins, favourite car, best match, per-car totals — in the garage, not buried in a diagnostics dialog. (M)
44. **Persistent rivalries** — personas wreck you and vanish at match end. Track head-to-head history per persona across sessions (times they wrecked you / you wrecked them) and surface a "nemesis" line in the garage and at match start ("Sal has wrecked you 6 times"). The roast engine already keys lines by persona pair — feed it the history. (M)
45. **A daily challenge** — one date-seeded challenge per day ("wreck 3 cars with the forklift today") with a streak counter on the menu. The standing challenges (round 1) are static; a daily is the classic reason to open the game tomorrow. (M)
46. **Seasons** — monthly: bests and mastery snapshot into a season card, season-only challenges appear, last season's card stays viewable. Gives the best-score system (round 1) a rhythm instead of an endless accumulation. (M)
47. **Scrap: a garage economy** — wrecks earn "scrap" (a number in localStorage, nothing purchasable with real money); spend it on cosmetic unlocks: extra paint finishes, horn sounds, nameplate styles. The garage already recolours cars; this gives the recolouring a reason and the wrecks a second currency besides score. (L)
48. **Medals on challenges** — round 1's seven challenges are binary ☆/★. Add bronze/silver/gold tiers (e.g. 25 props → 50 → 100 in a match) and a trophy shelf in the garage. (M)
49. **A match-history browser** — the PLAYLOG records are only visible as an aggregate table in the perf dialog. Make them browsable: list past matches (date, mode, car, score, wrecks), tap one for its full stats. The data is already written; this is presentation. (M)
50. **Family-yard weekly leaderboard** — publish each player's weekly best (score + wrecks) in presence while in the yard; the family panel shows the week's table. The family yard is the social centre of the game and currently keeps no score between sessions. (M)
51. **Unlockable cars** — all 15 cars are available from the first minute. Gate the exotic half (tiger, hellbender, bertha, combine, tanker) behind wreck/win milestones so the roster itself is a progression. Flagged as a design decision — if Jonas prefers everything open, this is the one to cut. (M)
52. **Shareable stats card** — render the career highlights (best score, nemesis, favourite car) to a canvas image with a Download/Share button — the play log already exports text for Claude; this is the version for humans. (S)
53. **Win streaks** — track consecutive match wins/top finishes, show the streak on the menu best line, and let the announcer mention a streak at 3+. (S)
54. **Best-score history** — round 1's bests are bare numbers. Keep the last 10 bests per mode+car with dates, so "your best" is a story ("2,450 — in the Viper, Nov 9") not just a ceiling. (S)
55. **Garage usage balance** — mastery stars (round 1) reward wrecks per car; add simple usage (matches/time per car) to the garage so a player can see the three cars they've never driven. (S)
56. **First-time ceremonies** — nothing marks a player's first Elimination win, first wreck, or first 1,000-point match beyond the moment itself. A one-time toast + a line in the career screen for each first. (S)
57. **Weekly challenge trio** — three rotating weekly challenges alongside the daily (bigger targets, e.g. "win an Elimination in three different cars this week"). (M)
58. **Nemesis bounty** — each week, the persona who wrecked you most (from #44's history) is marked as a bounty in matches: wrecking them pays bonus points, announced by Kev. Turns stored history into gameplay. (M)

## Content systems

59. **Ship the voice-clip pipeline** — `VCLIP` loads `voices/manifest.json` and the header comment says the clips are "made by voices/render.mjs", but the repo contains neither the folder nor the script: on the live deploy every line falls back to Web Speech, and the whole recorded-voice system (clip preference in `VCLIP.pick`, the voice-clip player) is dead code in production. Commit the render tool and a starter clip pack for the announcer + most-heard lines. (M)
60. **Announcer coverage gaps** — `barks.announce()` is called with ~40 events, but `ANN_LINES` has entries for only about half (present: wreck, bigHit, headOn, carFire, crushed, intoCrusher, underBall, rodeo, bull*, tankerBoom, burger, harvest, delivery, followUp, aiHit, youCrash, wheel…). Kev stays silent for `craneGrab`, `craneDrop`, `forkToss`, `forkLift`, `towHook`, `towWhip`, `shell`, `leak`, `sandwich`, `trackOff`, `turretOff`, `bentGun`, `bullGore`, `youWrecked` and more — exactly the special-weapon moments that deserve play-by-play. Write the missing pools. (M)
61. **A persona line-depth audit tool** — line counts per persona per event vary wildly (some personas have 1–2 lines for `wheel`/`low`); in a long match the thin events repeat audibly. A small script (node, reading the same literals) that prints the matrix and flags cells under 3 lines turns content-writing from guesswork into a checklist. (M)
62. **Cross-match roast memory** — `ROAST_PAIR`'s 30-second cooldown is per-session; the same Sal→Vinnie roast opens every match, every day. Persist recently-heard roasts (per pair, in localStorage) and deprioritise them for a few sessions. (S)
63. **New archetype: the motorcycle** — the roster is 15 four-wheeled boxes. A bike (single-track lean physics on the existing wheel system, very fast, very fragile, rider ragdoll-lite tumble on wreck) is the biggest roster shake-up available and reuses the damage/score systems wholesale. (L)
64. **New archetype: the tuk-tuk** — a three-wheeler: tippy, slow, comic, and a natural "hard mode" car. Cheap to build from existing parts (camper cabin + sandflea wheels) and instantly the family favourite to hunt. (M)
65. **Mid-match dynamic events** — hazards run on their own timers all match; nothing ever *changes*. Add an event scheduler: at announced moments the match mutates for 30 s — double points, crusher frenzy (faster slams), bull stampede (all bulls charge), lights-out (night + headlights). Telegraphed by Kev 5 s ahead. (M)
66. **Weather: rain** — there is no weather at all. A rain variant per match: grip scales down (the physics presets prove the knob exists), spray particles behind wheels, darker grade, wetter engine/crowd mix. One weather is enough to prove the system; wind gusts for light cars can ride the same switch later. (L)
67. **A living sky** — day/night is a fixed choice (and dusk, on the branch, a static preset). Let the sun drift across a long match (a 10-minute match crosses golden hour) so the yard's light is a clock you can feel. (S)
68. **MUSIC_FOR has drifted from the roster** — `MUSIC_FOR` still maps `mule`, `commuter` and `hearse` (cars that no longer exist) and has no entry for `dozer`, `bertha` or `combine`, which silently fall back to 'rock'. Give the three heavies their genres (dozer/bertha: dirge or march; combine: hillbilly writes itself) and delete the dead keys. (S)
69. **Two scoring scales for the same match** — the HUD/over screen use `game.score` (a wreck pays +1000) while `SCORE.pts()` computes k×100 − s×50 for the scoreboard: the same match shows two different "scores" in two places. Pick one scale (or label them honestly — "score" vs "league points") and make the over screen, scoreboard and bests agree. (M)
70. **Persona cards in the garage** — `CAR_CAST` carries `from`, style and colours for every driver, but the picker shows only the car. Show the driver card (who drives it, their style, their nemesis from #44) when a car is selected — the cast is the game's charm and it's currently invisible until they talk. (S)
71. **Session scars** — every match starts in a pristine yard; the previous match's flattened props and crater field reset. Keep prop damage flattened across matches within a session (props already track state; respawn only cars) so a long evening visibly accumulates. (S)
72. **Crowd wave** — `CROWD` bobs on an excitement value and the crowd bed (round 1) follows impact rate, but the crowd never *does* anything. Trigger a visible wave (and a chant swell) on player wrecks and lead changes. (M)
73. **Phase-aware music** — the soundtrack follows your car (`MUSIC_FOR`) but never the match: the last 30 seconds of a timed match and the last-two-standing of an Elimination sound like minute one. Add an escalation layer (tempo/density) keyed to match phase. (M)
74. **New archetype: the snowplow** — a working front blade (the fork/tow machinery shows the pattern for attachable front gear) that shoves cars and props without damaging itself: a control-space car the roster lacks between the forklift and the dozer. (M)
75. **Voice-clip coverage meter** — once clips exist (#59), `VCLIP.pick` silently falls back per line. Show coverage (clips vs TTS per persona/event) in the perf panel so missing clips are findable instead of audible. (S)
76. **Announcer duo** — `annPool()` splits `ANN_LINES` by index parity between Kev and SHAZZA: each match hears only one of them, on alternating halves of the same pools. Let them commentate together (play-by-play + colour) in the same match — the parity split is already the mechanism; it just needs to stop being either/or. (M)

## Creator & test tooling

77. **The update check only works on github.io** — `UPDATE.check()` bails unless the hostname ends in `github.io`, so the fernverk.com mirror (and any future custom domain) never learns a newer build exists and serves stale code forever. Make the check origin-agnostic: fetch the canonical Pages copy (or a tiny `version.json`) and compare `BUILD`. (S)
78. **A real test harness in the repo** — the repo has zero test files; the `__derby` surface exists "for console tinkering + tests" but nothing drives it. Add `tests/smoke.mjs` (headless Chrome via CDP: load, `startMatch`, run 600 frames, assert zero `ERRS`, fps floor, one wreck) so "run the tests" is a command, not a ritual. (M)
79. **CI on every push** — there is no `.github/workflows`. Add one: syntax-check the module, run the smoke harness (#78) headlessly, and fail the build badge on regression. Jonas's other-Claude workflow pushes builds blind today. (M)
80. **Deterministic seed mode** — spawns, props, AI choices and roasts all draw on `Math.random`, so a bug or a perf regression can never be reproduced exactly. Add `?seed=N` routing the game's randomness through a seeded PRNG (one choke point) — repros and before/after perf runs become comparable. (M)
81. **Play-log diff tool** — PERFREP/PLAYLOG exports are read by eye. A small script that takes two exports and prints the deltas (fps med/5%/1%, errors, phys fix/clamp, slow seconds per match) turns "did Build 68 get faster?" into a table. (M)
82. **Perf budgets as assertions** — the data exists (`PERFREP.summary`) but nothing ever fails. Define budgets per device class (e.g. desktop p5 ≥ 45 fps, zero blowups) and have the smoke run/autoplay report BUDGET FAIL lines in the play log. (S)
83. **VR telemetry is collected and thrown away** — `V.stats` counts clicks, buzzes, HUD and menu draws and is never read anywhere; `V.hz`, foveation and govern steps never reach PLAYLOG or PERFREP. Log a VR block (hz, quality steps, frame-miss estimate) into the match record so VR perf is diagnosable like everything else. (S)
84. **Bug reports need a picture** — `bugReport()` is text-only. Attach a downscaled canvas snapshot (the last rendered frame as JPEG) to the drop upload — half the bugs this game gets ("the car looked wrong") are visual. (M)
85. **Git hash in play-log records** — records carry the build number only; two builds with the same number (a hotfix re-push) are indistinguishable. Stamp the short commit hash next to `BUILD` in the record header. (S)
86. **TESTING.md** — autoplay, `?smoke`, `__derby`, PERFREP and the play-log workflow are documented only in code comments. One page in the repo so a future contributor (or a future Claude) can run the suite without reverse-engineering it. (S)
87. **Capture physics blowups** — the PHYS watchdog counts and clamps blowups (and round 1 got the count into PERFREP), but the event itself is unrecoverable. On a blowup, save the surrounding 2 s (car poses + velocities from the recorder) into the play log as a `blowup` event — the difference between "it exploded once" and a fixable repro. (M)
88. **Autoplay scenario matrix** — autoplay runs one shape of match (Respawn, `aplen` seconds, rotating cars). Add presets via URL (`?autoplay=elim`, `=full` 12 AI, `=lite` forced low quality tier) so a test session covers the shapes real players use. (M)
89. **The report drop is a public topic** — bug reports and play logs upload to a fixed public ntfy topic (`scrapyard-derby-f74277e052a2`) that anyone can subscribe to and read, device details included. Rotate the topic per release or gate it with a shared key in the filename; the convenience is worth keeping, the openness isn't necessary. (M)
90. **Name the transport in NETLOG** — the game silently picks among four transports (claude.ai room, PeerJS, ntfy relay, BroadcastChannel) and a failed one looks like "multiplayer is broken". Surface the active transport + fallback reason in the NETLOG header so any bug report says which road it took. (S)

## Accessibility & onboarding depth

91. **Full per-action key remapping** — *extends round-1 #94:* round 1 shipped two presets (WASD/IJKL). The remaining step is a remap UI: click an action, press a key, stored per action in localStorage, with conflict detection. (M)
92. **Reduce-flashing setting** — distinct from reduce-motion: fireworks bursts, the crusher's strobing warn light (`(clock*8)%1` flash), the red `#flash` damage vignette and bloom pulses are photosensitivity hazards that shake/vignette settings don't touch. One toggle that caps flash rates and kills strobes. (S)
93. **Visual sound cues** — crusher warnings, bull pawing, an approaching charge and the horn are audio-first. Add small directional icons at the screen edge for the three or four sounds that carry gameplay information (the hit-direction arc's machinery generalises). (M)
94. **UI scale for the HUD** — round 1 added a subtitle size; the HUD numerals, gauge labels and toasts are fixed sizes. One UI-scale setting (100/125/150%) applied to the HUD layer. (S)
95. **One-thumb touch mode** — the touch layout assumes two thumbs (stick + pedals + FIRE). Add auto-throttle (car drives at a capped speed, you steer and brake) as a one-thumb layout — also the natural mode for a kid holding a phone in one hand and a parent in the other. (M)
96. **Menu/HUD localisation** — every UI string is hard-coded English while the cast speaks five languages. Extract the menu/HUD strings and ship a first second language (Swedish — the family's other language) behind a picker. (L)
97. **A first-run garage tour** — main has no first-run detection at all; a new player lands on a menu with a car dock, two modes, multiplayer and 34 settings. Add a 30-second first-run overlay tour (pick a car → Start → these are your pedals) that shows once and never again. (M)
