# Scrapyard Derby — 100 new vehicle & character ideas, ranked

Ranked by fun for kids × how new the play feels × how distinct the character is. All original characters (archetypes only, no copies of real people or film characters).

1. **Baba Yaga's Hut** — a wooden cottage stomping on giant chicken legs; cackling Slavic folk witch; kicks and stomps cars; weakness: it's wood, so fire spreads fast.
2. **Mama Wanjiru's Robo-Elephant** — four-legged mech elephant; booming, laughing Kenyan safari ranger with Swahili ("Twende!", "Pole pole!"); trunk sprays water and flings cars; weakness: slow to turn.
3. **King Crab Kenji** — giant mechanical crab that walks sideways; shouting Osaka fish-market boss; claws grab and hold cars; weakness: soft belly if flipped.
4. **Abuela Araña** — six-legged spider crane that climbs walls; tiny fierce Argentine grandmother; webs cars to the floor with steel cable; weakness: legs snap under heavy rams.
5. **Zorp the UFO** — hovering saucer with a tractor beam that lifts cars and drops them; gibberish alien who mangles Earth words; weakness: hit from below, the beam fails.
6. **Captain Barnacle Bess** — pirate galleon on wheels; roaring Cornish pirate woman; cannons fire only sideways (broadsides); weakness: can't shoot forward.
7. **Jukka the Zamboni** — ice resurfacer that freezes the floor so everyone slides; deadpan Finnish hockey dad; weakness: melts in fire.
8. **Mister Sprinkles** — ice-cream van with a creepy jolly jingle; sing-song Welsh; scoops freeze tyres; weakness: the jingle gives it away.
9. **Dustin the Robo-Vac** — giant robot vacuum that sucks up debris and small cars; monotone Swedish; weakness: bumps into walls and full-bag clogs.
10. **Lek's Tuk-Tuk** — tiny three-wheeler that darts through gaps; horn-happy Bangkok driver ("Mai pen rai!"); weakness: tips over easily.
11. Henrietta the Hen-Walker — chicken-legged mech lays bomb eggs; Southern farm wife.
