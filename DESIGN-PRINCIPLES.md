# Scrapyard Derby: design principles

Read this before suggesting or building features. It comes from the owner.

1. **Simple, focused on the main play.** Improve the core fight: driving, crashing, weapons, AI, feel, sound, performance.
2. **No new modes, menus, settings or sliders** that make players choose things. Good defaults that just work beat options.
3. **Hidden conveniences are fine** if they cost the player nothing. For example, a gamepad just works, there's a key you can learn, and choices are remembered silently.
4. **When in doubt, leave it out**, or ask first.
5. **Every car has one strength and one visible signature weakness**, plus a counter car that beats it and prey that it beats. Weak spots show on the car itself (smoke, glow, leaks, a bent barrel) rather than as extra gauges on screen.
6. **Balance is judged by play-log metrics, not guesses.** The game logs every match: K/D per car, who wrecks whom, stuck spots, physics glitches and fps. Balance changes wait for that data.
7. **It's a kids' game.** Old-fashioned cussing and foreign cusses are OK; no F-bombs or slurs. Characters are original, with no imitation of real people.
8. **Never ship untested code**, and every bug gets a regression test.
