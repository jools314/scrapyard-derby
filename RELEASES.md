| 112 | 2026-11-17 | Faster start on weak laptops (owner: "work on the Chromebook startup too"). The Chromebook's first frame took 16 s. Its boot record showed why: the shader compile done while the menu is up built the wrong variants (the on-screen ones), but the yard is drawn through the post-processing buffer, which needs different ones, so the first frame then compiled ~90 programs again and froze; on the Chromebook the menu compile never even finished before its 8 s safety timeout. Now the menu, effect and countdown warm-ups compile the variants that are really drawn; the first frame draws only the sky behind the menu (3 programs instead of ~90) and the yard appears when its shaders are ready, without freezing the page; and a weak machine starts at the quality level it ended on last time. Measured with a 4x CPU slowdown: first frame median ~31 s -> 11-13 s. The boot record now also logs when the whole yard is on screen (`yard ms` in the report). Render/startup path only. Tests: new `fastboot` in quick; portalstart waits for the yard, and now also forces a quality step across the post-chain line in the menu and checks nothing compiles at Start. Also: when the quality governor steps across the post-processing line (quality 3 -> 4) or turns shadows off, every shader changes and the next frame compiled ~30 programs in one blocking step (a weak laptop froze just as it asked for speed, often right at Start; the first suite run caught it). Where the browser compiles in parallel, that set is now compiled in the background at once. |
